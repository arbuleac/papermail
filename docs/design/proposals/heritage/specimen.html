Paper design language · Heritage, rebuilt · v1.0

Wingbar

Sparrow’s 2011 shape — a dark slate account rail, a crisp bright list, one confident blue, compact avatar-forward rows — rebuilt with 2026 craft: device-pixel hairlines, a two-tier type hierarchy, rounded-square avatars and a dark mode tuned as carefully as the light one. The account rail is the wing; every row carries its account’s wingbar.

The list is the productFixed 68 pt rows, painted once, never animated. Speed is a visual property.
Slate frames, paper holdsDark chrome is identity and navigation; bright surfaces carry mail.
One blue, one meaningBlue means selected, unread or the primary action. Nothing else is blue.
Every state has a shapeDots, weights, wells and glyphs carry state; color only confirms it.
