The design language of Paper · direction “Kinetic minimal”

Specimen v1 · 2 Nov 2026
· one token set for AppKit, SwiftUI and the WKWebView reader
The key beats the pixelEvery action renders its end state in the same frame as the keystroke. Motion only confirms what already happened.
One flare per regionFlare marks where you are, what fires and what just happened. Each pane shows at most one Flare element at a time.
Say it twiceState always has two carriers: shape, weight, glyph or words, plus color. Never color alone.
Quiet until touchedHints, counts and chrome stay low until hover or focus raises them. Mail content is the loudest thing on screen.

Motion

Four durations, three curves. Keys complete any running animation at once; nothing longer than 250 ms.
0 msselection, focus, hover in
90 mssnap: press, hover out, dot
140 msquick: toast, wing morph
200 msmove: row fly-out, collapse

Elevation

Rows and collapsed cards are flat. Shadows appear only on things that float above the content.
